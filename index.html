Aegon

Redesigning the way savers engage with their pension.

The context.

Jan 2025

Aegon, the UK's largest digital savings platform, wanted to introduce visual investment performance in their recently launched mobile app, to increase engagement and retention.

The target audience: job switchers. Recently changed jobs, consolidating old pots or curious about their pension's growth, and deciding whether to trust Aegon with their money.

Design principles.

01 Understand the moment. Acknowledge their life moments. Make them feel understood, not judged.
02 Build confidence. Offer clear, reliable information that makes decisions feel easier, not heavier.
03 Be transparent. Avoid hidden meaning, jargon, or anything that could feel uncertain.
04 Keep it simple. Ease of use is everything. Reduce the time between comprehension and action.
05 Respect their time. Offer short, actionable guidance that fits into a busy life.

Exploration. Three directions, one decision.

I explored three directions before committing. Each answered the brief differently.

Tested
A
Annotated line
The safe play. Traditional line chart with markers for key events. Answers what happened, not what it means.
B
Comparison-first
Always show you vs. a benchmark. Stronger story, but forces a comparison the user might not want, and benchmarks are messy in pensions.
Tested Chosen
C
Direct-manipulation
A single clean line with segmented timeframe controls, plus scrubbing to reveal value at any point. Standard controls for the big picture, direct manipulation for the detail.

I chose C because it answered the primary question and the secondary ones, without cluttering the default view. The tradeoff was discoverability: interaction-led patterns are harder to learn. I'll come back to how I solved that.

User testing improvements.

June 2025, with our user researcher. What triggers a check? Do people understand what they see? Where does comprehension break?

Insight 01 · Low financial literacy
"The graph says it's going up, but the return is negative… I have no idea what it might mean."
Insight 02 · Misleading windows
A negative 'return this year' made users think the pension wasn't doing well overall.
Insight 03 · High financial literacy
"I can clearly see we're missing percentage values."
Insight 04 · Triggers
"There was a big plummet in the stock market recently, checking to see if it's recovered."

Three main findings shaped the final solution: Events drive visits. Short windows mislead. Numbers need context.

The scenario. Are my savings in good hands?

A job switcher, a few months in, opens the app to explore their pension. One question underneath it all: is my money being looked after, and is it growing?

Ninety seconds. One thumb. The chart's job is reassurance over reaction: they should leave understanding that, over time, their savings are earning.

Research showed events like market dips trigger visits. We deliberately designed against panic: longer timeframes show the long arc, and the disclaimer keeps short-term noise in context.

The result. Designed as an instrument, not a picture.

Interaction. Two controls, two levels of detail.

Segmented control
Four timeframes, one tap: 6M, 1Y, 3Y, All time. The standard pattern; no learning curve.
Scrubbing
Drag to reveal value at any point in time: direct manipulation for the question segments can't answer.

Rich interaction from minimal input. The discipline was knowing where not to invent: two patterns, two clear jobs.

Choosing the timeframes was the harder design.

A learning vehicle: the right timeframes depend on who is looking.

SegmentSuggested timeframesWhy it works
New joiners 1M · 3M · 1Y Early wins, builds trust, habit formation.
Active savers 1Y · 3Y · 5Y Reinforces progress, scenario planning, upsell nudges.
Deferred users Since last contribution · 5Y · to retirement Re-engages "dormant but growing", supports consolidation.

Longer horizons: later engagement, greater retention. Which set to ship became the hardest decision. I'll come back to it.

Handoff. A design isn't done until an engineer can build it without asking.

The accessibility spec was externally audited by User Vision, whose guidance centred on three areas: the non-sighted experience, tap target standards, and axis guidance.

Figma spec table · Investment chart v1.2
Replace with: assets/images/handoff-spec.png

A real extract from the handoff documentation

1
Hit area extends 24px
The visual line is 2px. The interactive hit area is 24px tall. Without the extension, the chart looks tappable but isn't, especially on smaller phones. Verified against User Vision's tap-target guidance.
2
Number is the primary loss signal
A colour-blind user perceives the muted red as grey. That's fine: the minus sign and bold weight carry the meaning. Colour is reinforcement, not signal.
3
TalkBack summarises bands, not points
The first spec read out start, end, peak and low values with their dates. We evolved it to summarise bands of movement: faster to listen to, and closer to how a sighted user actually scans the line. Equivalence, not translation.

The hardest decision. The one I almost got wrong.

Selecting the timeframes.

The instinct was to design for everyone. That usually means designing for no one.

Option A, the new-joiner set. 1M · 3M · 1Y. Early wins, fast engagement. The PRD's original v1 spec.

Option B, longer horizons. 6M · 1Y · 3Y · All time. Truer to what a pension is.

The two timeframe options, side by side

Reflection. What I'd do differently.

I'd put the timeframe debate in front of real users earlier. We settled it with judgement and shipped data; a week of testing would have settled it with evidence.

The transferable principle: designing trust into data-dense moments matters anywhere users make decisions under uncertainty, whether that's choosing a fund or choosing what to watch next.

What's next.

Three things earned a place on the roadmap: a benchmark overlay (the comparison direction B wanted, done as an opt-in rather than a default), projection to retirement for deferred savers, and segment-adaptive timeframes once the audience matures beyond the launch cohort.
